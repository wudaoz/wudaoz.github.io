*This is a preview. The full post is coming soon.*

In traditional security, a threat model starts from a clear picture: the attacker is outside, the system is inside, and there is a trust boundary between them. AI agents blur that picture. The agent sits inside the boundary and acts with the user's permissions, yet it can be steered by the data it reads, pursue a goal that is slightly off, or simply make a mistake. From the outside, all three look like the same command.

In this post I want to ask the old questions again for this new setting: who is the attacker, what are we protecting, and where should the trust boundary go?
