About Me

Hi, my name is Weiheng Wu (吴伟恒). I received my M.S. degree in Cyberspace Security from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). My M.S. research focused on intrusion detection systems for network security.

I am currently a Research Engineer working with Prof. David Lo (ACM and IEEE Fellow) at Singapore Management University (SMU). My recent research focuses on security issues in AI and agents.
